# White Chicken Chili

Serves 4 at 1× · Prep 10m · Cook 30m · Total 40m

Tags: dinner, soup, chicken

## Sources

From a single source.

**A — Ambitious Kitchen — Healthy White Chicken Chili**
<https://www.ambitiouskitchen.com/healthy-white-chicken-chili/>
*the whole recipe — blended chickpeas thicken it instead of cream*

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **½ tablespoon** olive oil
- **1 medium** white onion, chopped
- **1 can** mild green chiles (or 2 seeded, diced jalapeños)
- **1 tablespoon** ground cumin
- **2 teaspoons** chili powder
- **¼ teaspoon** coriander
- **¼ teaspoon** dried oregano
- **4 cups** low sodium chicken broth
- **2 (15 ounce) cans** chickpeas, rinsed and drained
- **¾ teaspoon** salt, plus more to taste
- freshly ground black pepper
- **1½ pounds** boneless skinless chicken thighs (or breast)
- **½ cup** frozen corn
- **1 medium** lime, juiced
- **⅓ cup** fresh cilantro, chopped

### For garnish

- tortilla strips or chips
- extra cilantro
- avocado slices
- lime wedges

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high. Cook the onion and green chiles until soft, 3–4 minutes.
2. Stir in the cumin, chili powder, coriander and oregano for 30 seconds. Add the broth, one can of chickpeas, the raw chicken, salt and pepper, and simmer on medium-low for 20–30 minutes.
3. Lift out the chicken, shred it with two forks and return it to the pot with the lime juice and cilantro.
4. Blend the second can of chickpeas with ½ cup water until fairly smooth.
5. Stir the blended chickpeas and the corn into the pot and simmer 5–10 minutes more. Serve with the garnishes.

## Notes

Our verdict: it was OK but missing something. The broth had good flavor, but it wasn't spicy at all and felt a bit thin on substance.

To try next time: - more veg (it's just onion and chiles as written) - more corn - more heat — jalapeños instead of the mild chiles, or extra chili powder.   If we do, pull Coop's portion before the extra heat goes in.
