# Sausage, Potato & Kale Soup (Zuppa Toscana)

Serves 6 at 1× · Prep 15m · Cook 30m · Total 45m

Tags: dinner, soup

## Sources

Blended from 2 sources. Ingredients below are marked with the letter of the source they came from.

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **1 pound** Italian sausage — *A*
  - *we skip the bacon, onion and garlic — the sausage has a ton of flavor on its own*
- **1/2 – 1 teaspoon** red pepper flakes — *A*
- **2 teaspoon** Italian seasoning — *A*
- **1½ teaspoon** salt, plus more to taste — *A*
- **⅛ teaspoon** cracked black pepper, plus more to taste — *A*
- **3 tablespoon** arrowroot starch — *A*
- **4 cups** diced russet potatoes — *A*
- **32 oz** low sodium chicken broth — *A*
- **1** zucchini, diced — *B*
  - *our addition — more veg in the pot*
- **1 cup** full-fat coconut milk — *A*
  - *the recipe is already dairy-free — we keep this and just never add cream*
- **4 cups** curly kale — *A*

## Method

1. Heat a large stockpot over medium. Add the sausage and brown it, breaking it up as it cooks.
2. Stir in the red pepper flakes, Italian seasoning, salt and pepper and cook a minute or two.
3. Sprinkle the arrowroot over the meat and stir to coat. Add the potatoes and broth, bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer about 30 minutes, until the potatoes are tender. Add the zucchini for the last 10 minutes or so.
4. Stir in the coconut milk and kale and simmer just until the kale wilts. Serve right away.

## Notes

Ease: 1/6 (our rating, 1 = easiest).

We leave out the recipe's 4 strips of bacon, 1 onion and 6 cloves of garlic — the sausage has a ton of flavor and doesn't need them — and add a zucchini. No dairy: the coconut milk stays (it isn't dairy), we just don't add cream.

The red pepper flakes are the only added heat; use the low end if Coop is eating it.

Reheats well for lunch.
