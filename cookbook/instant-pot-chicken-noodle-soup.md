# Instant Pot Chicken Noodle Soup

Serves 6 at 1× · Total 45m

Tags: dinner, soup, instant-pot, chicken

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **1 tablespoon** olive oil — *A*
- **5 medium** carrots, cut into 1/4-inch-thick diagonal slices — *A*
- **3 large stalks** celery, cut into 1/2-inch-thick slices — *A*
- **2 cloves** garlic, minced — *A*
- **1 large** yellow onion, cut into a large dice — *A*
- extra vegetables — *B*
  - *we always add more veggies than written — no set amount, more carrots and celery or whatever's in the fridge*
- kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper — *A*
- **1** 3-pound whole chicken — *A*
- **1** 3-inch piece ginger, halved lengthwise (optional) — *A*
- **6 ounces** extra-wide egg noodles (about 4 cups) — *A*
  - *cooked in their own pot, not in the soup — see method*
- **2 tablespoons** dill fronds, roughly chopped — *A*

## Method

1. Set the Instant Pot to high sauté. Add the oil, then the carrots, celery, garlic, onion, any extra vegetables, 1 tablespoon salt and some pepper. Cook 4–5 minutes, until just starting to soften.
2. Add the whole chicken, the ginger if using, and 8 cups water. Lock the lid and pressure cook on high for 20 minutes.
3. Quick release the pressure. Discard the ginger and lift the chicken out to cool for a few minutes.
4. Meanwhile, cook the noodles in a separate pot of salted boiling water until al dente, 5–7 minutes, and drain. (The recipe cooks them in the broth on high sauté — we don't, so they don't soak up the broth, especially in leftovers.)
5. Shred the chicken into bite-size pieces, discarding the skin and bones. Season it, then return it to the pot with the dill. Adjust the seasoning.
6. Put noodles in each bowl and ladle the soup over.

## Notes

Ease: 3/6 (our rating, 1 = easiest).

We add extra veggies, and cook the noodles separately to save the broth — noodles left in the soup soak it all up. Store leftover noodles and soup separately.
