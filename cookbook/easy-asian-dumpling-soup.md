# Easy Asian Dumpling Soup

Serves 4 at 1× · Prep 5m · Cook 15m · Total 20m

Tags: dinner, soup

From a single source.

**A — Hello Little Home — Easy Asian Dumpling Soup**

*the whole recipe — store-bought dumplings in a quick vegetable broth*

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **6 cups** vegetable broth
- **1 large** carrot, peeled and thinly sliced
- **¼ head** cabbage, chopped
- **1 tablespoon** soy sauce
- **1 teaspoon** sesame oil
- sriracha sauce, to taste
  - *added at the table, after Coop's bowl is served*
- **1 16-ounce package** dumplings (Thai vegetable gyoza recommended)
- **2** green onions, thinly sliced (green part only)

## Method

1. Bring the broth to a boil in a medium saucepan over high heat.
2. Add the carrot and cabbage and return to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer until tender, 5–10 minutes.
3. Stir in the soy sauce and sesame oil; taste and add more soy sauce if needed.
4. Drop the dumplings straight into the simmering broth and cook per the package directions, about 5 minutes. (The recipe cooks them separately — boiling them in the broth is easier and saves a pot.)
5. Ladle into bowls and top with green onions. Serve Coop's, then add sriracha to the rest to taste.

## Notes

Ease: 6/6 (our rating, 1 = easiest).

We boil the dumplings right in the broth to make it super easy.

About 10–12 dumplings feeds the two of us, with the full 6 cups of broth.

The only heat is the sriracha — leave it out of the pot and add it to bowls after Coop's is served.
