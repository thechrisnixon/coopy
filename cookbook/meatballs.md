# Meatballs

Serves 4 at 1× · Prep 20m · Cook 20m · Total 40m

Tags: dinner, beef, pasta, contains-dairy

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

### Meatballs

- **1 lightly packed cup** diced white sandwich bread, crusts removed
- **1 small** onion (brown, white or yellow)
- **14 oz** ground beef (400 g)
- **3 oz** ground pork (100 g), or more beef
- **1** egg
- **¼ cup** fresh parsley, finely chopped
- **2** garlic cloves, minced
- **¼ cup** Parmigiano-Reggiano, freshly grated
- **¾ tsp** salt
- **¼ tsp** black pepper

### Cooking meatballs & sauce

- **2½ tbsp** olive oil
- **2** garlic cloves, minced
- **¾ cup** onion, finely chopped
- **24 oz** tomato passata (700 g)
- **½ cup** water
- **½ tsp** red pepper flakes (can omit for not spicy)
- **3 tsp** dried Italian herb mix
- **1 tsp** cooking salt / kosher salt
- black pepper

### To serve

- pasta of choice
- Parmesan
- parsley, finely chopped (optional)

## Method

1. Grate the onion on a box grater into a large bowl — you want about ½ cup of onion and juice. Stir in the bread so it soaks up the juice and falls apart, and leave it 5 minutes.
2. Add the rest of the meatball ingredients and mix well by hand.
3. Roll heaped tablespoons into balls.
4. Heat 1½ tbsp oil in a large non-stick pan over medium-high and brown the meatballs all over, 3–4 minutes. They should not be cooked through. Move to a plate.
5. Add the remaining 1 tbsp oil, then the onion and garlic, and cook 2–3 minutes until translucent. Add the rest of the sauce ingredients, bring to a simmer, and turn to medium-low so it bubbles gently.
6. Return the meatballs and their juices to the sauce and cook 8–10 minutes, turning occasionally. Season to taste. Cook the pasta meanwhile.
7. Serve over pasta with extra Parmesan and parsley.
