# Blueberry Banana Waffles

Prep 3m · Cook 5m · Total 8m

Tags: coop, breakfast, waffles

From a single source.

<https://feedingtinybellies.com/waffles-for-babies-blueberry-banana-waffles/>
*the whole recipe — a blender oat batter sweetened only by banana and berries*

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **1 large** overripe banana
- **1 cup** old fashioned rolled oats
- **½ tsp** ground cinnamon
- **1 tsp** baking powder
- **2** eggs
- **2 tbsp** mild-tasting oil
- **¼ tsp** vanilla extract
- **⅓ cup** blueberries

## Method

1. Heat the waffle iron.
2. Blend everything except the blueberries until smooth.
3. Stir in the blueberries.
4. Pour a small amount of batter into the iron and cook according to the waffle maker's directions.
5. Serve whole or sliced into strips.

## Notes

Makes 8 mini waffles.
