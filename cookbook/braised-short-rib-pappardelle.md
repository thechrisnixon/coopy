# Braised Short Rib Pappardelle

Serves 10 at 1× · Prep 25m · Cook 3h · Total 3h 30m

Tags: dinner, beef, pasta, freezes-well, contains-dairy, special, postpartum-freezer

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

### For the short rib ragu

- **4 pounds** English cut beef short ribs
- **3 tablespoons** olive oil, divided
- **3 large** carrots, peeled and diced
- **1 large** yellow onion, finely diced
- **1 stalk** celery, finely diced
- **8 cloves** garlic, finely chopped or grated
- **6 ounces** tomato paste
- **1 cup** dry red wine
- **1 0.75-ounce package** fresh "poultry herb blend" (or about 2 sprigs rosemary, 4 sprigs sage and 12 sprigs thyme)
- **2** bay leaves
- **1** parmesan rind (optional)
- **14 ounces** crushed tomatoes
- **2 cups** low-sodium beef broth/stock or water
- kosher salt and ground black pepper, to season

### For the pasta

- **24 ounces** pappardelle or pasta of choice
- **½ cup** heavy cream
- **½ cup** grated parmesan
- to serve — grated parmesan, chopped fresh herbs, as desired

## Method

1. Pat the short ribs dry and season with a heaping tablespoon of kosher salt and pepper. Heat oil in a large heavy pot over medium-high and brown the ribs in batches, 3–4 minutes per side. Set aside and pour off all but 2 tablespoons of fat.
2. Over medium, add the carrots, onion and celery with 1 teaspoon salt and some pepper and cook, stirring now and then, until deeply browned, 15–20 minutes.
3. Add the garlic and stir 1–2 minutes until fragrant, then the tomato paste for 2–3 minutes until it darkens.
4. Raise the heat to medium-high, pour in the wine and scrape up the browned bits. Cook 3–4 minutes until almost all absorbed.
5. Tie the herbs with twine and add them with the bay leaves and parmesan rind. Add the crushed tomatoes, broth and short ribs. Bring to a boil, then cover and simmer on low, stirring occasionally, 2½–3 hours until the meat falls apart. Add a splash of water and lower the heat if it reduces too fast.
6. Lift out the ribs, discard the herbs, bay leaves and rind, shred the meat off the bones, and stir it back in. The ragu can be cooled and stored at this point.
7. To serve, cook the pasta in salted water until al dente, saving about 1 cup of pasta water; don't rinse. Meanwhile bring the ragu to a simmer and stir in the cream and parmesan over low.
8. Toss the pasta into the ragu over medium for 1–2 minutes, loosening with pasta water or tightening with more parmesan. Serve topped with parmesan and herbs.

## Notes

A special-occasion dinner. The braised short rib ragu is also on our postpartum freezer list — freeze it after shredding the meat back in, before the cream and parmesan, and finish it with the pasta on the day.

The source serves 10–12.
