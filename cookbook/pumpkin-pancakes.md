# Pumpkin Pancakes

Prep 5m · Cook 15m · Total 20m

Tags: coop, breakfast, pancakes, freezes-well, contains-dairy

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **½ cup** pumpkin puree
- **2 large** eggs
- **½ cup** white whole wheat flour
- **2 tsp** baking powder
- **½ tsp** pumpkin pie spice or cinnamon (optional)
- **⅛ tsp** salt
- **2-3 tbsp** milk (optional)
- cooking oil or spray

## Method

1. Whisk the pumpkin puree and eggs together in a medium bowl.
2. Add the flour, baking powder, spice and salt on top and stir gently until just combined. Loosen with milk a tablespoon at a time if the batter is too thick.
3. Oil a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Cook 1 tablespoon of batter per pancake for 3-4 minutes, until golden underneath with bubbles at the edges, then flip and cook 2-3 minutes more. Repeat with the rest of the batter.
4. Serve.

## Notes

Makes 12-14 small pancakes. Freezes well.

Keeps in an airtight container in the fridge for 5 days, or frozen for up to 3 months. Reheat in the microwave or toaster.
