# Winter Minestrone

Serves 8 at 1× · Cook 45m · Total 2h

Tags: dinner, soup, chicken, spicy

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **3 medium** red onions, chopped — *A*
- **4 ribs** celery, chopped — *A*
- **2 medium** carrots, chopped — *A*
- **⅓ cup** extra-virgin olive oil — *A*
  - *with no pancetta, this is all the fat in the soffritto*
- **1 bunch** Swiss chard, stems and leaves separated — *A*
- **6** garlic cloves, finely chopped — *A*
- **1 teaspoon** salt, plus more to taste — *A*
- **¾ teaspoon** pepper — *A*
- **2 tablespoons** tomato paste — *A*
- **1 28-ounce can** whole tomatoes in juice — *A*
- **3 quart** hot water — *A*
- **5 cups** coarsely chopped cored Savoy cabbage (6 ounces) — *A*
- **5 cups** coarsely chopped escarole (1/2 pound) — *A*
- **1 19-ounce can** cannellini beans, rinsed and drained — *A*
- spicy chicken sausage, sliced — *B*
  - *our addition instead of the pancetta — use mild for Coop's portion, or pull his before the sausage goes in; our call each time*
- **1** zucchini, chopped — *B*
  - *our addition*

### To serve

- extra-virgin olive oil, for drizzling — *A*
- cooked ditalini tossed with oil (optional) — *A*

## Method

1. Brown the sausage in a splash of the oil in a wide 7- to 9-quart heavy pot over medium, then lift it out and set aside.
2. Add the rest of the oil, the onions, celery and carrots, and cook over medium, stirring now and then, while you prep the chard: cut out the stems and chop them, keeping the leaves separate.
3. Stir in the chard stems, garlic, salt and pepper. Keep cooking, stirring occasionally, until everything is very soft and starting to stick to the pot — about 45 minutes in all. This slow soffritto is where the flavor comes from.
4. Push the vegetables aside, add the tomato paste to the bare spot and cook, stirring, about 2 minutes until it starts to caramelize. Stir it through the vegetables and cook 2 minutes more without letting it burn.
5. Add the tomatoes and their juice, breaking them up, then the 3 quarts hot water, scraping up the browned bits.
6. Bring to a simmer, add the cabbage and escarole, cover and simmer about 40 minutes, until the greens are tender.
7. Chop the chard leaves and stir them in with the beans, zucchini and browned sausage. Simmer, partly covered, 10 minutes. Season with salt and pepper. Stir in ditalini just before serving, if using.

## Notes

We leave out the recipe's 1/3 pound pancetta, the Parmigiano-Reggiano rind and the grated parmesan to serve, and add spicy chicken sausage and a zucchini.

The sausage is the only heat. Use mild sausage for Coop's portion, or pull his before the spicy sausage goes in — whichever suits that night.
