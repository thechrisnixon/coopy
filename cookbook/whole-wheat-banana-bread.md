# Whole Wheat Banana Bread

Prep 10m · Cook 55m · Total 1h 5m

Tags: baking, snack

**A — The Seasoned Mom — Whole Wheat Banana Bread**
<https://www.theseasonedmom.com/the-perfect-whole-wheat-banana-bread/>
*the whole recipe — a one-bowl loaf, half whole wheat, lightened with applesauce*

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **1½ cups** mashed very ripe bananas (about 3 large or 4 smaller)
  - *we've made it with just 1 big banana and it's fine*
- **¼ cup** unsweetened applesauce
- **2 tablespoons** salted butter, melted
- **½ cup** sugar
- **1 large** egg
- **1 teaspoon** vanilla extract
- **1 teaspoon** baking soda
- **¼ teaspoon** salt
- **¾ cup** whole wheat flour
- **¾ cup** all-purpose flour

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 350°F and spray an 8½ × 4½-inch loaf pan.
2. In a large bowl, stir together the bananas, applesauce and melted butter.
3. Stir in the sugar, egg and vanilla until smooth.
4. Sprinkle the baking soda and salt over the batter and stir them in.
5. Add both flours and stir just until no dry streaks remain — don't overmix. Fold in any mix-ins (chocolate chips, nuts, blueberries) now.
6. Pour into the pan and bake 50–60 minutes, until golden and a toothpick in the center comes out clean. Tent with foil if the top browns too fast.
7. Cool in the pan 5–10 minutes, then turn out onto a rack and cool completely before slicing.

## Notes

OK to make with 1 big banana instead of the full 1 ½ cups.

Makes one loaf, about 12 slices.
