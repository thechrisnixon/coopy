# Skyline Chili

Serves 8 at 1× · Prep 20m · Cook 2h 30m · Total 2h 50m

Tags: cincinnati, chili, freezes-well, weekend

## Sources

Blended from 2 sources. Ingredients below are marked with the letter of the source they came from.

**A — The Chunky Chef — Copycat Skyline**
<https://www.thechunkychef.com/copycat-skyline-cincinnati-chili/>
*the chocolate, the sugar, and the heavy chili powder — what makes it read as Skyline rather than generic chili*

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **2 lb** lean ground beef — *B*
  - *crumbled in raw and never browned — both sources insist, and it's what gives the fine, sauce-like texture*
- **6 cups** water — *B*
  - *between the two — a scales to 8 cups at this batch size, b calls for 4; 6 reduces to the right consistency in 2½ hours*
- **6 oz** tomato paste — *A*
  - *the one line both sources agree on exactly — one small can*
- **½ oz** unsweetened baking chocolate — *A*
  - *the signature. b's card predates it entirely and is noticeably flatter without it*
- **¼ cup** chili powder — *A*
  - *b calls for 2 tbsp; a scales to 6½. A quarter cup splits it and is where it tastes right*
- **2 large** yellow onions, chopped — *B*
  - *a uses none at all — this is most of what b is for*
- **2** garlic cloves — *B*
  - *the card says "1 toe garlic," left whole and fished out at the end — two, minced, is better*
- **1½ tsp** ground cinnamon — *B*
- **1½ tsp** ground cumin — *A*
- **1 tsp** ground allspice — *B*
  - *b runs heavy here (1½ tsp) against a's ¼ — b is right, this is a Cincinnati marker*
- **½ tsp** ground cloves — *A*
  - *absent from b entirely*
- **1 tsp** Worcestershire sauce — *B*
- **3** whole bay leaves — *B*
- **2 tsp** kosher salt — *A*
  - *b's full tablespoon is too much at this batch size*
- **½ tsp** black pepper — *B*
- **½ tsp** red pepper flakes — *B*
- **1 tsp** sugar — *A*
  - *not in b — rounds off the vinegar*
- **2 tbsp** cider vinegar — *A*

### To serve

- **1 lb** thin spaghetti
- **8 oz** mild cheddar, finely shredded
- oyster crackers

## Method

1. Heat the water in a large pot over medium and whisk in the tomato paste and the chocolate until both fully dissolve, about 3 minutes. Doing this before the meat goes in is a's contribution — it stops the paste from clumping.
2. Add the chili powder, cinnamon, cumin, allspice, cloves, black pepper, red pepper flakes, salt, sugar, Worcestershire and vinegar. Stir to combine.
3. Crumble the raw beef straight into the pot with your fingers, then work it apart with a potato masher or whisk until no lumps remain. Do not brown it first — this is the whole trick.
4. Add the onions, garlic and bay leaves. Bring to a boil.
5. Drop to a low simmer and cook uncovered for 2½ hours, stirring every half hour, until it has reduced by about a third and the fat has risen.
6. Pull the bay leaves. Taste and adjust salt and vinegar.
7. Serve over thin spaghetti under a mound of finely shredded cheddar — a three-way. Oyster crackers on the side.

## Notes

Better on day two, and noticeably better on day three. Make it the day before you want to eat it.

Chill overnight and lift the fat cap off before reheating if you want it cleaner, but leave some — it carries the spice. Reheat gently; boiling breaks the texture.

Freezes well in 2-cup portions.

The card is scaled for 10–12 and has kitchen conversions scribbled across the top in pen: 4 C = 1 qt, 2 C = 1 pt.
