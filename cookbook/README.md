# ABC Muffins (Apple, Banana & Carrot)

Prep 10m · Cook 16m · Total 26m

Tags: coop, snack, muffins, freezes-well

## Sources

From a single source.

**A — Yummy Toddler Food — ABC Baby Muffins (Apple, Banana and Carrot!)**
<https://www.yummytoddlerfood.com/baby-muffins/>
*the whole recipe — extra-moist mini muffins with three kinds of produce*

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **1 cup** mashed very ripe banana (about 2–3 small bananas)
- **½ cup** shredded apple (about 1 small apple)
- **¼ cup** shredded carrot (about 1 small carrot)
- **¾ cup** milk
- **¼ cup** melted unsalted butter, slightly cooled
- **1 large** egg
- **1 teaspoon** pure vanilla extract
- **1 cup** whole wheat flour
- **½ cup** rolled oats
- **1 teaspoon** cinnamon
- **1 teaspoon** baking powder
- **½ teaspoon** baking soda
- **⅛ teaspoon** salt

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 375°F and spray a 24-cup mini muffin pan.
2. Stir the banana, apple, carrot, milk, butter, egg and vanilla together in a medium bowl.
3. Gently but thoroughly stir in the flour, oats, cinnamon, baking powder, baking soda and salt.
4. Fill each mini cup to the rim — about 2 tablespoons of batter each.
5. Bake 16–20 minutes, until a tester comes out clean (the source does 18).
6. Cool a few minutes in the pan, then move to a rack. Dice for little hands or serve whole.

## Notes

Makes 24 mini muffins (or 12 regular). They freeze well.

They're meant to be moister inside than a normal muffin, so they're easy for a baby to chew.

# Banana Spinach Pancakes

Prep 2m · Cook 10m · Total 20m

Tags: coop, breakfast, pancakes, freezes-well

## Sources

From a single source.

**A — Creative Nourish — Banana Spinach Pancakes Baby Recipe**
<https://www.creativenourish.com/spinach-pancakes/>
*the whole recipe, a blender batter of banana, oats, egg and spinach*

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **1 ripe** banana
- **½ cup** oats
- **2** eggs
- **½ cup** raw spinach
- **3 tablespoon** water
- coconut or avocado oil, for cooking

## Method

1. Blend everything except the oil until smooth.
2. Heat a skillet over medium heat with a little oil. Drop the batter in tablespoons, about 4 pancakes at a time, and cook for about 3 minutes, until the edges are golden and set enough to flip easily.
3. Flip and cook for another 2 minutes.
4. Serve plain or with toppings — nut butter and berries both work.

## Notes

Makes 12 mini pancakes. Freezes well.

Keeps in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days, or frozen for up to 3 months with parchment between layers. Reheat from frozen in a 180°C / 350°F oven for 5-10 minutes, in the toaster for 1 minute on the defrost setting, or in the microwave for 15 seconds on defrost.

# Blueberry Carrot Oatmeal Bars

Prep 10m · Cook 24m · Total 34m

Tags: coop, snack

## Sources

From a single source.

**A — Yummy Toddler Food — Easy Oatmeal Bars (with Blueberry and Carrot)**
<https://www.yummytoddlerfood.com/easy-oatmeal-bars/>
*the whole recipe — one-bowl oat bars with almond butter, maple and grated carrot*

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **¾ cup** quick or instant oats (or rolled oats ground in a blender)
- **½ cup** whole wheat flour
- **½ teaspoon** baking powder
- **½ teaspoon** cinnamon
- **¼ teaspoon** salt
- **¼ cup** melted coconut oil (or butter)
- **¼ cup** creamy unsweetened almond butter
- **¼ cup** maple syrup
- **1** egg (optional)
  - *without it the bars are crisp like a cookie; with it they're cakier*
- **½ cup** grated carrot
- **½ cup** blueberries, halved if very large

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Line an 8×8-inch pan with parchment, leaving an overhang on two sides to lift the bars out later.
2. Mix everything except the blueberries in a medium bowl (egg too, if using), then gently fold in the berries.
3. Press evenly into the pan, right to the edges.
4. Bake 24–26 minutes, until the edges are lightly golden and set.
5. Let it cool completely — the fridge speeds this up. Cut warm and it crumbles.
6. Lift out and cut into 16 small squares, or larger if you like.

## Notes

Makes 16 small bars. Good made ahead.

# Braised Short Rib Pappardelle

Serves 10 at 1× · Prep 25m · Cook 3h · Total 3h 30m

Tags: dinner, beef, pasta, freezes-well, contains-dairy, special, postpartum-freezer

## Sources

From a single source.

**A — Plays Well With Butter — Slowly Braised Beef Short Rib Ragu**
<https://playswellwithbutter.com/short-rib-ragu-recipe/>
*the whole recipe — short ribs braised into a ragu, finished with cream and parmesan*

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

### For the short rib ragu

- **4 pounds** English cut beef short ribs
- **3 tablespoons** olive oil, divided
- **3 large** carrots, peeled and diced
- **1 large** yellow onion, finely diced
- **1 stalk** celery, finely diced
- **8 cloves** garlic, finely chopped or grated
- **6 ounces** tomato paste
- **1 cup** dry red wine
- **1 0.75-ounce package** fresh "poultry herb blend" (or about 2 sprigs rosemary, 4 sprigs sage and 12 sprigs thyme)
- **2** bay leaves
- **1** parmesan rind (optional)
- **14 ounces** crushed tomatoes
- **2 cups** low-sodium beef broth/stock or water
- kosher salt and ground black pepper, to season

### For the pasta

- **24 ounces** pappardelle or pasta of choice
- **½ cup** heavy cream
- **½ cup** grated parmesan
- to serve — grated parmesan, chopped fresh herbs, as desired

## Method

1. Pat the short ribs dry and season with a heaping tablespoon of kosher salt and pepper. Heat oil in a large heavy pot over medium-high and brown the ribs in batches, 3–4 minutes per side. Set aside and pour off all but 2 tablespoons of fat.
2. Over medium, add the carrots, onion and celery with 1 teaspoon salt and some pepper and cook, stirring now and then, until deeply browned, 15–20 minutes.
3. Add the garlic and stir 1–2 minutes until fragrant, then the tomato paste for 2–3 minutes until it darkens.
4. Raise the heat to medium-high, pour in the wine and scrape up the browned bits. Cook 3–4 minutes until almost all absorbed.
5. Tie the herbs with twine and add them with the bay leaves and parmesan rind. Add the crushed tomatoes, broth and short ribs. Bring to a boil, then cover and simmer on low, stirring occasionally, 2½–3 hours until the meat falls apart. Add a splash of water and lower the heat if it reduces too fast.
6. Lift out the ribs, discard the herbs, bay leaves and rind, shred the meat off the bones, and stir it back in. The ragu can be cooled and stored at this point.
7. To serve, cook the pasta in salted water until al dente, saving about 1 cup of pasta water; don't rinse. Meanwhile bring the ragu to a simmer and stir in the cream and parmesan over low.
8. Toss the pasta into the ragu over medium for 1–2 minutes, loosening with pasta water or tightening with more parmesan. Serve topped with parmesan and herbs.

## Notes

A special-occasion dinner. The braised short rib ragu is also on our postpartum freezer list — freeze it after shredding the meat back in, before the cream and parmesan, and finish it with the pasta on the day.

The source serves 10–12.

# Broccoli Beef

Serves 4 at 1× · Prep 17m · Cook 13m · Total 30m

Tags: dinner, beef, special

## Sources

From a single source.

**A — Natasha's Kitchen — Beef and Broccoli**
<https://natashaskitchen.com/beef-and-broccoli/>
*the whole recipe — flank steak and broccoli in a ginger-garlic soy sauce*

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

### Beef and broccoli

- **1 lb** flank steak, very thinly sliced into bite-sized strips
- **2 Tbsp** olive oil (or vegetable oil), divided
- **1 lb** fresh broccoli (about 6 cups of florets)
- **2 tsp** sesame seeds, optional garnish

### Stir fry sauce

- **1 tsp** fresh ginger, grated (loosely packed)
- **2 tsp** garlic, grated (from 3 cloves)
- **½ cup** hot water
- **6 Tbsp** low sodium soy sauce (or GF tamari)
- **3 Tbsp** packed light brown sugar
- **1½ Tbsp** corn starch
- **¼ tsp** black pepper
- **2 Tbsp** sesame oil

## Method

1. Get the rice going first. Freeze the steak for 30 minutes so it slices easily, then cut it thinly against the grain.
2. Stir all the sauce ingredients together until the sugar dissolves.
3. Heat 1 Tbsp oil over medium and cook the broccoli, partly covered and stirring now and then, 4–5 minutes until bright green and crisp-tender. Remove.
4. Turn the heat to high, add the other 1 Tbsp oil, and sear the beef in a single layer, 2 minutes per side, until cooked through.
5. Pour in the sauce, drop to medium-low and simmer 3–4 minutes until thick. Return the broccoli and toss, adding 1–2 Tbsp water if it's too thick. Serve over rice with sesame seeds.

# Cauliflower Mac n Cheese

Serves 4 at 1× · Prep 10m · Cook 15m

Tags: coop, dinner, contains-dairy

## Sources

From a single source.

**A — Real Little Meals — Cauliflower Mac N Cheese**
<https://reallittlemeals.com/post/cauliflower-mac-n-cheese/>
*the whole recipe, a cheese sauce thickened with blended cauliflower*

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **8 oz** elbow pasta
- **2 cups** cauliflower
- **1¼ cups** milk
  - *split — 1/2 cup goes into the cauliflower purée, 3/4 cup into the sauce*
- **1½ cup** cheddar cheese
- **2 tbsp** butter
- **1 tbsp** flour
- **1 tsp** garlic powder
- **1 tsp** dijon mustard
- salt and pepper, to taste

## Method

1. Cook the pasta according to the package directions.
2. Steam the cauliflower.
3. Blend the steamed cauliflower with 1/2 cup of the milk until smooth.
4. Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat, add the flour, and whisk into a roux. Whisk in the remaining 3/4 cup milk and bring to a boil.
5. Turn the heat to low and stir in the cheese, cauliflower purée, garlic powder, dijon, salt and pepper until the cheese has melted.
6. Toss the sauce with the cooked pasta.

## Notes

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for 2-3 days. Reheat on the stovetop or in the microwave.

# Cherry Tomato White Wine Pasta

Serves 4 at 1× · Prep 5m · Cook 40m · Total 1h

Tags: dinner, chicken, contains-dairy

## Sources

Blended from 2 sources. Ingredients below are marked with the letter of the source they came from.

**A — NYT Cooking — Lemon and Garlic Chicken With Cherry Tomatoes (Martha Rose Shulman)**
<https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1016151-lemon-and-garlic-chicken-with-cherry-tomatoes>
*the recipe — pounded lemon-garlic chicken and a white wine cherry tomato pan sauce*

**B — Our changes**
*store-bought pre-marinated lemon herb chicken instead of marinating our own, and serving it over rigatoni and cauliflower gnocchi*

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

### Marinade

- **2 tablespoons** extra-virgin olive oil — *A*
  - *skip the whole marinade when we use pre-marinated chicken*
- **3 tablespoons** lemon juice — *A*
- **2** garlic cloves, minced or puréed — *A*
- **1 teaspoon** fresh rosemary, chopped — *A*
- salt and black pepper, to taste — *A*

### Chicken

- **2** boneless skinless chicken breasts, pre-marinated lemon herb — *B*
  - *we buy pre-marinated lemon herb chicken to make this very easy — the source marinates plain breasts in the lines above*
- **2 tablespoons** all-purpose flour (or a gluten-free flour such as rice or corn flour) — *A*
- **2 tablespoons** grapeseed, sunflower or canola oil — *A*

### Sauce

- **¼ cup** dry white wine — *A*
  - *we triple this*
- **2 heaped cups** cherry tomatoes (about ¾ pound) — *A*
  - *we triple this*
- pinch of sugar — *A*
  - *we triple this*
- **1 tablespoon** flat-leaf parsley, chopped — *A*
- **¼ cup** grated Parmesan (optional) — *A*

### To serve

- **1 lb** rigatoni — *B*
  - *the sauce goes over pasta — that's why we call it pasta*
- **1 bag** frozen cauliflower gnocchi — *B*
  - *cooked separately and tossed with the rigatoni — Amanda and Cooper like the blend*

## Method

1. If marinating your own: mix the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, rosemary, salt and pepper, halve each chicken breast (thirds if 12 oz or more), and refrigerate in the marinade 15–30 minutes. With pre-marinated chicken, just cut it into pieces.
2. Pat the chicken dry. Between two oiled sheets of plastic wrap, pound each piece from the center out to about ¼ inch thick, gently so it doesn't tear.
3. Season one side with salt and pepper, dredge lightly in flour, and tap off the excess.
4. Turn the oven on low. Heat the oil in a wide heavy skillet over high and cook the chicken a piece or two at a time without crowding, about 1½ minutes per side, until browned in spots. Keep warm in the oven. Pour off all but about a tablespoon of fat.
5. Drop the heat to medium-high, add the wine and scrape up the browned bits. Add the tomatoes and cook, tossing often, until they start to shrivel and burst. Add the sugar, salt and pepper and cook 5–10 minutes more, until collapsed but still whole.
6. Meanwhile, boil the rigatoni and cook the cauliflower gnocchi separately per its package, then toss them together.
7. Spoon the tomatoes over the chicken and pasta, scatter with parsley (and Parmesan if using), and serve.

## Notes

We call this "cherry tomato white wine pasta" — the sauce is the point for us. We triple the sauce lines (wine, tomatoes, sugar) and keep the chicken as written. The quantities above are the source's 1X.

Buying pre-marinated lemon herb chicken makes this very easy: skip the marinade and go straight to pounding.

# Chicken Bolognese

Serves 4 at 1× · Total 1h 40m

Tags: dinner, chicken, pasta, freezes-well, contains-dairy, spicy, postpartum-freezer

## Sources

Blended from 2 sources. Ingredients below are marked with the letter of the source they came from.

**A — Food Network — Katie Lee Biegel's Chicken Bolognese**
<https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/katie-lee/chicken-bolognese-9493573>
*the whole sauce — evaporated milk and white wine instead of the classic cream and red*

**B — Our changes**
*Calabrian chilis stirred in at the end, after Coop's portion comes out*

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **2 tablespoons** olive oil — *A*
- **1 pound** ground chicken breast (or a mix of dark and white) — *A*
- **1 large** carrot, finely chopped — *A*
- **½** onion, finely chopped — *A*
- **2 cloves** garlic, minced — *A*
- **1 tablespoon** chili powder — *A*
- **1 teaspoon** Italian seasoning — *A*
- **1** bay leaf — *A*
- **1 6-ounce can** tomato paste — *A*
- **½ cup** dry white wine — *A*
- **½ cup** evaporated milk — *A*
- **2 8-ounce cans** tomato sauce (or one 16-ounce can) — *A*
- **1½ teaspoons** kosher salt, plus more as needed — *A*
- **½ teaspoon** freshly ground black pepper — *A*
- Calabrian chilis, chopped — *B*
  - *stirred in at the very end, after we've taken Coop's portion out — he doesn't do spicy*
- **1 pound** rigatoni — *A*
- **2 tablespoons** fresh parsley, chopped — *A*
- grated Parmesan, for serving — *A*

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-high and brown the chicken, breaking it up, 5–7 minutes. Add the carrot and onion and cook until tender, 7–8 minutes.
2. Add the garlic, chili powder, Italian seasoning and bay leaf and toast 1–2 minutes, then stir in the tomato paste and cook until it darkens, 1–2 minutes.
3. Pour in the wine and simmer until it's absorbed. Add the evaporated milk and stir constantly until it cooks down.
4. Add the tomato sauce, salt, pepper and ½ cup water. Cover and simmer over medium, stirring now and then, until thick, 45 minutes to 1 hour. Fish out the bay leaf.
5. Take out Coop's portion now, then stir the Calabrian chilis into the rest of the sauce.
6. Meanwhile cook the rigatoni in salted water until al dente, saving some pasta water. Toss the pasta with the sauce, loosening with pasta water as needed. Top with parsley and serve with Parmesan.

## Notes

Pretty good! We triple the sauce and freeze it — it's on our postpartum freezer list.

When we triple, we only use about 2/3 of a bag of pasta for two meals.

Coop's portion comes out before the Calabrian chilis go in; the chili powder in the base isn't hot.

# Chicken Lettuce Wraps

Serves 4 at 1× · Prep 30m · Cook 20m · Total 50m

Tags: dinner, chicken

## Sources

Blended from 2 sources. Ingredients below are marked with the letter of the source they came from.

**A — The Kitchn — Chicken Lettuce Wraps**
<https://www.thekitchn.com/how-to-make-chicken-lettuce-wraps-cooking-lessons-from-the-kitchn-220124>
*the whole recipe — hoisin sauce, ground chicken, mushrooms and water chestnuts*

**B — Our changes**
*more vegetables — a bell pepper and a zucchini cooked in with the mushrooms*

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

### Sauce

- **3 to 5 tablespoons** hoisin sauce — *A*
  - *start with 3, add more at the end to taste*
- **2 tablespoons** soy sauce — *A*
- **2 tablespoons** rice vinegar — *A*
- **1 teaspoon** toasted sesame oil — *A*
- **1 teaspoon** cornstarch (optional, for a glossier sauce) — *A*
- **1 pound** ground chicken — *A*
- **2 teaspoons** vegetable oil, divided — *A*
- **8 ounces** white button or cremini mushrooms, finely diced — *A*
- **1** bell pepper, thinly sliced — *B*
  - *our addition — goes in with the mushrooms*
- **1** zucchini, diced — *B*
  - *our addition — goes in with the mushrooms*
- **1 (8-ounce) can** water chestnuts, drained and finely chopped — *A*
- **3 cloves** garlic, minced — *A*
- **1 tablespoon** fresh ginger, peeled and minced — *A*
- **½ cup** scallions, thinly sliced (about 6), divided — *A*

### To serve

- **2 small heads** Bibb or butter lettuce — *A*
- sriracha or red pepper flakes, on the side — *A*

## Method

1. Whisk 3 tbsp hoisin with the soy sauce, rice vinegar and sesame oil (and the cornstarch, if using). Keep it by the stove.
2. Heat 1 tsp oil in a large skillet over medium. Cook the chicken, breaking it into small pieces, until no longer pink, 7–8 minutes. Move it to a bowl.
3. Add the remaining 1 tsp oil to the pan. Cook the mushrooms, bell pepper and zucchini, stirring now and then, until tender, 4–5 minutes.
4. Stir in the water chestnuts, garlic and ginger and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
5. Return the chicken with half the scallions, add the sauce, and cook until bubbling and warmed through, 30–60 seconds. Taste and add more hoisin if it needs it.
6. Serve with the lettuce leaves, the rest of the scallions, and sriracha or pepper flakes on the side, for spooning into leaves at the table.

## Notes

We add a thinly sliced bell pepper and a zucchini along with the mushrooms.

The heat is all at the table, so the filling is Coop-friendly as is.

The filling keeps 4 days in the fridge. Reheat just what you need, with a splash of broth if it looks dry.

# Chicken Pot Pie Casserole

Serves 8 at 1× · Prep 20m · Cook 40m · Total 1h

Tags: dinner, chicken, contains-dairy, postpartum-freezer

## Sources

From a single source.

**A — The Real Food Dietitians — Chicken Pot Pie Casserole**
<https://therealfooddietitians.com/chicken-pot-pie-casserole/>
*the whole recipe — a one-dish pot pie filling under cheddar drop biscuits*

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

### For the filling

- **¼ cup** unsalted butter
- **1 medium** yellow onion, diced
- **1 16-oz bag** frozen mixed vegetables (corn, carrots, peas, green beans)

### Dough

- **2¼ teaspoons** quick-rise (instant) or active dry yeast (one ¼-ounce package)
- **¼ cup** granulated sugar (50 g)
- **4 tablespoons** salted butter, melted and cooled (57 g)
- **1 large** egg, room temperature
- **1 large** egg yolk, room temperature
- **3 cups** bread flour (360 g), plus more as needed
- **¾ teaspoon** kosher salt
- extra-virgin olive oil, for greasing the bowl

### Filling

- **4 tablespoons** salted butter, softened (57 g)

- **4 oz** cream cheese, softened (112 g)

## Method

### Chantilly cream

- **16 ounces** cream cheese, softened
- **8 ounces** unsalted butter, softened

- **1 Tablespoon** vanilla extract

- **1 lb** thin spaghetti
- **8 oz** mild cheddar, finely shredded
- oyster crackers

## Method

1. Heat the water in a large pot over medium and whisk in the tomato paste and the chocolate until both fully dissolve, about 3 minutes. Doing this before the meat goes in is a's contribution — it stops the paste from clumping.
2. Add the chili powder, cinnamon, cumin, allspice, cloves, black pepper, red pepper flakes, salt, sugar, Worcestershire and vinegar. Stir to combine.
3. Crumble the raw beef straight into the pot with your fingers, then work it apart with a potato masher or whisk until no lumps remain. Do not brown it first — this is the whole trick.
4. Add the onions, garlic and bay leaves. Bring to a boil.
5. Drop to a low simmer and cook uncovered for 2½ hours, stirring every half hour, until it has reduced by about a third and the fat has risen.
6. Pull the bay leaves. Taste and adjust salt and vinegar.
7. Serve over thin spaghetti under a mound of finely shredded cheddar — a three-way. Oyster crackers on the side.

## Notes

Better on day two, and noticeably better on day three. Make it the day before you want to eat it.

Chill overnight and lift the fat cap off before reheating if you want it cleaner, but leave some — it carries the spice. Reheat gently; boiling breaks the texture.

<https://www.epicurious.com/recipes/food/views/winter-minestrone-351167>
*the base — a long-cooked soffritto, browned tomato paste and a pot full of winter greens, no broth needed*

**B — Our changes**

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **3 medium** red onions, chopped — *A*
- **4 ribs** celery, chopped — *A*
- **2 medium** carrots, chopped — *A*
- **⅓ cup** extra-virgin olive oil — *A*
  - *with no pancetta, this is all the fat in the soffritto*
- **1 bunch** Swiss chard, stems and leaves separated — *A*
- **6** garlic cloves, finely chopped — *A*
- **1 teaspoon** salt, plus more to taste — *A*
- **¾ teaspoon** pepper — *A*
- **2 tablespoons** tomato paste — *A*
- **1 28-ounce can** whole tomatoes in juice — *A*
- **3 quart** hot water — *A*
- **5 cups** coarsely chopped cored Savoy cabbage (6 ounces) — *A*
- **5 cups** coarsely chopped escarole (1/2 pound) — *A*
- **1 19-ounce can** cannellini beans, rinsed and drained — *A*
- spicy chicken sausage, sliced — *B*
  - *our addition instead of the pancetta — use mild for Coop's portion, or pull his before the sausage goes in; our call each time*
- **1** zucchini, chopped — *B*
  - *our addition*

### To serve

- extra-virgin olive oil, for drizzling — *A*
- cooked ditalini tossed with oil (optional) — *A*

## Method

1. Brown the sausage in a splash of the oil in a wide 7- to 9-quart heavy pot over medium, then lift it out and set aside.
2. Add the rest of the oil, the onions, celery and carrots, and cook over medium, stirring now and then, while you prep the chard: cut out the stems and chop them, keeping the leaves separate.
3. Stir in the chard stems, garlic, salt and pepper. Keep cooking, stirring occasionally, until everything is very soft and starting to stick to the pot — about 45 minutes in all. This slow soffritto is where the flavor comes from.

## Ingredients

- **1½ teaspoons** kosher salt, plus more for the pasta water and to taste
- **8 ounces** spaghetti (225 grams)

- **4** garlic cloves, minced

- handful of fresh basil leaves, cut into thin ribbons

## Method

1. Cook the spaghetti in well-salted boiling water until 1 minute short of done. Save 1 cup of the pasta water, then drain.

6. Toss in half the parmesan and basil, then top with the rest to serve.
