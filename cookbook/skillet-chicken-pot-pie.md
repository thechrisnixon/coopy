# Skillet Chicken Pot Pie

Serves 6 at 1× · Prep 10m · Total 1h

Tags: dinner, chicken, contains-dairy

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **1 tbsp.** olive oil
- **3** boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into 1" chunks
- kosher salt
- freshly ground black pepper
- **½** onion, chopped
- **2 medium** carrots, peeled and chopped
- **2 stalks** celery, chopped
- **2 tsp.** fresh thyme
- **3 tbsp.** flour
- **1½ c.** frozen peas
- **2 c.** chicken broth
- **2 tbsp.** heavy cream
- **1 can** refrigerated biscuit dough
- **1** egg, lightly beaten

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 350°. Heat the oil in a large ovenproof skillet over medium. Season the chicken with salt and pepper and cook 6–8 minutes, until golden all over. Set it aside.
2. Add the onion, carrots, celery and thyme to the skillet and cook 4–5 minutes, until soft. Sprinkle in the flour and cook 2–3 minutes more.
3. Pour in the broth and simmer 8–10 minutes, until slightly thickened. Turn off the heat and stir in the cream, peas and chicken.
4. Split each biscuit in half horizontally and lay them, overlapping, in a ring around the edge of the skillet. Brush with the egg and bake 25–30 minutes, until golden.

## Notes

The recipe says it serves 6–8.

Contains dairy (heavy cream).
