# Cherry Tomato White Wine Pasta

Serves 4 at 1× · Prep 5m · Cook 40m · Total 1h

Tags: dinner, chicken, contains-dairy

## Sources

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

### Marinade

- **2 tablespoons** extra-virgin olive oil — *A*
  - *skip the whole marinade when we use pre-marinated chicken*
- **3 tablespoons** lemon juice — *A*
- **2** garlic cloves, minced or puréed — *A*
- **1 teaspoon** fresh rosemary, chopped — *A*
- salt and black pepper, to taste — *A*

### Chicken

- **2** boneless skinless chicken, pre-marinated lemon herb (we use thighs) — *B*
  - *we buy Whole Foods' Kitchens lemon herb boneless thighs (2 packs) to make this very easy — the source marinates plain breasts in the lines above*
- **2 tablespoons** all-purpose flour (or a gluten-free flour such as rice or corn flour) — *A*
- **2 tablespoons** grapeseed, sunflower or canola oil — *A*

### Sauce

- **¼ cup** dry white wine — *A*
  - *we triple this*
- **2 heaped cups** cherry tomatoes (about ¾ pound) — *A*
  - *we triple this*
- pinch of sugar — *A*
  - *we triple this*
- **1 tablespoon** flat-leaf parsley, chopped — *A*
- **¼ cup** grated Parmesan (optional) — *A*

### To serve

- **1 lb** rigatoni — *B*
  - *the sauce goes over pasta — that's why we call it pasta*
- **1 bag** frozen cauliflower gnocchi — *B*
  - *cooked separately and tossed with the rigatoni — Amanda and Cooper like the blend*

## Method

1. If marinating your own: mix the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, rosemary, salt and pepper, halve each chicken breast (thirds if 12 oz or more), and refrigerate in the marinade 15–30 minutes. With pre-marinated chicken, just cut it into pieces.
2. Pat the chicken dry. Between two oiled sheets of plastic wrap, pound each piece from the center out to about ¼ inch thick, gently so it doesn't tear.
3. Season one side with salt and pepper, dredge lightly in flour, and tap off the excess.
4. Turn the oven on low. Heat the oil in a wide heavy skillet over high and cook the chicken a piece or two at a time without crowding, about 1½ minutes per side, until browned in spots. Keep warm in the oven. Pour off all but about a tablespoon of fat.
5. Drop the heat to medium-high, add the wine and scrape up the browned bits. Add the tomatoes and cook, tossing often, until they start to shrivel and burst. Add the sugar, salt and pepper and cook 5–10 minutes more, until collapsed but still whole.
6. Meanwhile, boil the rigatoni and cook the cauliflower gnocchi separately per its package, then toss them together.
7. Spoon the tomatoes over the chicken and pasta, scatter with parsley (and Parmesan if using), and serve.

## Notes

We call this "cherry tomato white wine pasta" — the sauce is the point for us. We triple the sauce lines (wine, tomatoes, sugar) and keep the chicken as written. The quantities above are the source's 1X.

Buying pre-marinated lemon herb chicken makes this very easy: skip the marinade and go straight to pounding.
