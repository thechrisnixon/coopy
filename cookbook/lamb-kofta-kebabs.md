# Lamb Kofta Kebabs

Serves 4 at 1× · Prep 16m · Cook 9m · Total 25m

Tags: dinner, lamb, beef, special

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **1 pound** ground lamb and ground beef, mixed — *B*
  - *the source is all lean lamb (beef as a sub) — we do part beef, part lamb*
- **1 medium** Spanish onion, minced — *A*
- **1 clove** garlic, minced — *A*
- **¼ cup** curly parsley, finely chopped — *A*
- **½ teaspoon** salt — *A*
- **¼ teaspoon** ground pepper — *A*
- **¼ teaspoon** cinnamon — *A*
- **¼ teaspoon** allspice — *A*
- **⅛ teaspoon** ground cloves — *A*

## Method

1. Heat the grill to medium-high. Knead the meat with the onion, garlic, parsley, salt, pepper, cinnamon, allspice and cloves until the seasoning is evenly through it.
2. Divide into 4 portions (6 for our 1½× batch) and shape each into an 8-inch sausage around a heavy metal skewer.
3. Right before the meat goes on, oil the grate with a paper towel soaked in oil and held in tongs.
4. Grill, turning every 3–4 minutes, until cooked through, 9–13 minutes. Rest 4 minutes before sliding off the skewers.

## Notes

We make it 1½× (1½ lb meat, part beef and part lamb) for 6 kebabs, which is enough for 4 meals. Use the 1.5× scaler.
