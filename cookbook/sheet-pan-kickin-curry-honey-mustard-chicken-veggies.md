# Sheet Pan Kickin' Curry Honey Mustard Chicken & Veggies

Serves 4 at 1× · Prep 15m · Cook 30m · Total 45m

Tags: dinner, sheet-pan, chicken

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

### For the chicken and veggies

- **1½ pounds** boneless skinless chicken thighs — *A*
- **2 cups** 1-inch diced yukon gold or baby red potatoes (or sweet potatoes) — *A*
- butternut squash, cubed — *B*
  - *we add butternut squash and roast it with the potatoes and carrots*
- **3 large** carrots, sliced into ½ inch thick chunks (or 6 small heirloom rainbow carrots) — *A*
- **1** yellow onion, cut into chunks — *A*
- **8 ounces** brussels sprouts, halved, outer yellow leaves removed — *A*

### For the kickin' curry honey mustard

- **¼ cup** honey — *A*
- **¼ cup** dijon mustard (grainy is good) — *A*
- **2 tablespoons** olive oil — *A*
- **1 tablespoon** yellow curry powder — *A*
- **¼ teaspoon** cayenne pepper (optional, for a bit of heat) — *A*
- salt and lots of freshly ground black pepper — *A*

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Line one large or two small baking sheets with parchment or foil.
2. Whisk the honey, mustard, olive oil, curry powder, cayenne, salt and pepper in a bowl.
3. Toss the potatoes, butternut squash and carrots with some of the sauce, spread them on the pan in their own sections, and roast for 15 minutes on their own — they take longer than the chicken.
4. Meanwhile toss the chicken, onion and brussels sprouts with the rest of the sauce. Add them to the pan in their own sections and season with more salt and pepper.
5. Bake 25–35 minutes more, rotating the pan and stirring the veg halfway, until the potatoes and carrots are fork tender and the chicken reaches 165°F.
6. Garnish with chopped parsley if you like and serve right away.

## Notes

We start the potatoes, butternut squash and carrots 15 minutes before the chicken and brussels sprouts go in, so everything finishes together.

The cayenne is optional and the sauce isn't hot without it — leave it out and this works for Coop as is.
