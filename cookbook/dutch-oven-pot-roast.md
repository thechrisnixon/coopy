# Dutch Oven Pot Roast

Serves 6 at 1× · Prep 30m · Cook 3h 30m · Total 4h

Tags: dinner, beef, special

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **3 pounds** beef chuck roast, boneless — *A*
- **1½ teaspoons** kosher salt, plus more to taste — *A*
- **1 teaspoon** ground black pepper — *A*
- olive oil — *A*
- **2 large** yellow onions, peeled and sliced — *A*
- **6 cloves** garlic, minced — *A*
- **2 tablespoons** tomato paste — *A*
- **1 cup** red wine (or beef broth) — *A*
- **2 to 3 cups** beef broth, plus more as needed — *A*
- **2 teaspoons** Worcestershire sauce — *A*
- **4 sprigs** fresh thyme — *A*
- **2 sprigs** fresh rosemary — *A*
- **2** bay leaves, dried — *A*
- **1 pound** baby carrots — *A*
- extra carrots — *B*
  - *we add extra carrots beyond the pound*
- parsnips — *B*
  - *we add parsnips — they go in with the carrots and potatoes*
- **1½ pounds** baby yellow potatoes — *A*
- chopped Italian parsley, for garnish (optional) — *A*

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 300°F. Season the beef generously all over with the salt and pepper.
2. Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a 7–8 quart Dutch oven over medium-high and sear the beef until golden, 5–6 minutes per side. Set aside.
3. Turn to medium, add the onions and cook until browned, about 3 minutes, scraping up the bottom. Add the garlic for 1 minute.
4. Add the wine to deglaze, then 2 cups broth, the Worcestershire, tomato paste, thyme, rosemary and bay leaves. Bring to a low boil.
5. Return the beef — the liquid should come about halfway up it. Cover and bake 1½ hours at 300°F.
6. Add the carrots, parsnips and potatoes, cover again, and bake 2 hours more until fork-tender, plus 15–30 minutes if you want it softer still.
