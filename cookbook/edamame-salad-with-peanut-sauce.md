# Edamame Salad with Peanut Sauce

Serves 6 at 1× · Prep 20m · Total 20m

Tags: lunch, salad, make-ahead

## Sources

From a single source.

**A — Nora Cooks — Edamame Salad with Peanut Sauce**
<https://www.noracooks.com/edamame-salad/>
*the whole recipe — edamame, crunchy vegetables and a peanut-maple dressing*

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

### Salad

- **16 ounces** frozen shelled edamame
- **1 cup** red cabbage, shredded
- **1 cup** carrots, shredded
- **½ cup** green onions, chopped
- **2 cups** kale, chopped
- **¼ cup** cilantro, chopped (optional)
- **½ cup** peanuts (or cashews), chopped

### Peanut sauce

- **6 tablespoons** creamy peanut butter
- **3 tablespoons** seasoned rice vinegar
- **3 tablespoons** low-sodium soy sauce
- **3 tablespoons** pure maple syrup
- **1 teaspoon** toasted sesame oil
- **¼ teaspoon** ground ginger
- **1 teaspoon** Sriracha (or to taste)
  - *we leave it out of the sauce so it works for Coop — Sriracha on the side*

## Method

1. Cook or thaw the edamame per the package, then rinse under cold water.
2. Put the edamame in a large bowl with the rest of the salad ingredients.
3. Whisk the sauce ingredients until smooth, adding a splash of warm water if it's too thick.
4. Pour the sauce over the salad and toss well.
5. Top with extra chopped nuts if you like. It's best chilled for at least an hour before serving.

## Notes

Makes a good make-ahead lunch: it keeps in the fridge for a few days.
