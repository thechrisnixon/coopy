# Monster Muffins (Spinach Banana)

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **1 cup** sliced ripe banana (about 2 small or 1 medium/large)
- **2 cups** lightly packed baby spinach
- **¾ cup** milk (dairy or unsweetened plain nondairy)
- **¼ cup** honey
- **2 tablespoons** melted and cooled butter (or neutral oil, or plain yogurt)
- **2** eggs
- **1 teaspoon** vanilla extract
- **1 cup** rolled oats
- **1 teaspoon** baking soda
- **⅛ teaspoon** salt
- **1 cup** whole-wheat flour
- mini chocolate chips (optional)

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 375°F and grease a standard 12-cup muffin tin well.
2. Put everything except the flour and chocolate chips in a blender and blend until completely smooth and green, like a smoothie, scraping down the sides as needed.
3. Pulse or gently stir in the flour, just until combined.
4. Fill the cups about three-quarters full and sprinkle with chocolate chips, if using.
5. Bake 18–20 minutes, until firm to the touch and lightly browned at the edges.
6. Loosen with a paring knife if needed and cool on a rack.

## Notes

Makes 12 muffins. Serve warm, at room temperature, or cold from the fridge; they also keep in the freezer.
