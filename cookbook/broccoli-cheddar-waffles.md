# Broccoli Cheddar Waffles

Prep 10m · Cook 10m

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **½ cup** cheddar cheese, shredded
- **¼ cup** broccoli, chopped
- **1** egg
- **¼ tsp** garlic powder

## Method

1. Stir the cheddar, broccoli, egg and garlic powder together in a bowl until evenly combined.
2. Heat a mini waffle iron and lightly grease it with cooking spray or oil once hot.
3. Spoon in the batter and spread it evenly, close the lid, and cook for 3-4 minutes, until golden brown and crisp.
4. Let cool before serving.

## Notes

One batch makes 2 waffles. We usually make it 2× for 4 waffles, and freeze the extras.

Keeps in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days, or in the freezer for up to 2 months. Reheat in the toaster or toaster oven until warmed through.
