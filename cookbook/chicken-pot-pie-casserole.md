# Chicken Pot Pie Casserole

Serves 8 at 1× · Prep 20m · Cook 40m · Total 1h

Tags: dinner, chicken, contains-dairy, postpartum-freezer

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

### For the filling

- **¼ cup** unsalted butter
- **1 medium** yellow onion, diced
- **1 16-oz bag** frozen mixed vegetables (corn, carrots, peas, green beans)
- **½** flour (gluten-free works)
- **4 cups** low-sodium chicken broth
- **1 teaspoon** fine salt
- **½ teaspoon** dried thyme or dried parsley
- **½ teaspoon** garlic powder
- **¼ teaspoon** black pepper
- **1 lb** chopped boneless skinless chicken breast or thighs
  - *raw or pre-cooked both work*

### For the biscuits

- **2 cups** flour (gluten-free works)
- **1 tablespoon** baking powder
- **1 teaspoon** garlic powder
- **½ teaspoon** baking soda
- **¼ teaspoon** fine salt
- **6 tablespoons** cold unsalted butter
- **1 cup** shredded cheddar cheese, divided
- **1½ teaspoons** dried thyme or dried parsley
- **1 cup** dairy or unsweetened non-dairy milk
- **1 teaspoon** apple cider vinegar or white wine vinegar
- flaky sea salt

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 400℉ and spray a 9×13-inch (3-quart) baking dish.
2. Melt the butter in a Dutch oven or large pot over medium-high. Cook the onion and frozen vegetables 5–6 minutes, until tender. Add the flour, toss to coat, and cook 1 minute more, stirring.
3. Turn the heat to low and add the broth a little at a time, stirring after each addition. Stir in the salt, thyme, garlic powder and pepper. Raise the heat to medium-high and boil 2–3 minutes until it thickens to a gravy.
4. Drop to medium-low, add the chicken and cook 5–7 minutes, until it turns opaque — it finishes in the oven. Spread in the baking dish and bake, uncovered, 10 minutes.
5. Meanwhile, whisk the 2 cups flour, baking powder, garlic powder, baking soda and salt. Cut in the cold butter until it looks like coarse crumbs. Stir in ¾ cup of the cheddar and the thyme, then the milk and vinegar, just until a soft, slightly sticky dough forms.
6. Pull the casserole out and drop the dough over the top in 1–2 tablespoon mounds. It doesn't need to be fully covered. Bake 15 minutes.
7. Scatter the remaining ¼ cup cheddar and the flaky salt over the biscuits and bake another 8–10 minutes, until browned.
8. Rest at least 10 minutes before serving; the gravy thickens as it cools.

## Notes

Ease: 5/6 (our rating, 1 = easiest).

On our postpartum freezer list. We portion it for lunches in small foil containers.

Contains dairy (butter and cheddar in the biscuits).

Leftovers keep, covered, in the fridge up to 4 days.
