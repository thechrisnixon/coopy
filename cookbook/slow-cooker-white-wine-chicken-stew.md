# Slow Cooker White Wine Chicken Stew

Serves 4 at 1× · Prep 20m · Cook 7h 15m · Total 7h 35m

Tags: dinner, slow-cooker, chicken

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **1 tablespoon** olive oil
- **1½ pounds** boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1 inch pieces
- freshly ground salt and pepper
- **2 cups** chicken broth
- **½ cup** dry white wine
- **1 tablespoon** worcestershire sauce
- **1 teaspoon** balsamic vinegar
- **2 teaspoons** chopped fresh rosemary
- **2 teaspoons** chopped fresh thyme
- **½ teaspoon** dried oregano
- **1 teaspoon** salt, plus more to taste
- lots of freshly ground black pepper
- **6** garlic cloves, finely minced
- **1 large** yellow onion, cut into chunks
- **3 large** carrots, peeled and cut into ½-inch diagonal slices
- **1 pound** yukon gold potatoes, diced into ½ inch cubes
- **¼ cup** all purpose flour (or gluten-free substitute)
- **½ cup** unsweetened almond milk
- **⅔ cup** frozen peas

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a large skillet over high heat. Season the chicken with salt and pepper and sear 5–6 minutes, just to brown it — it doesn't need to cook through. Move it to the slow cooker.
2. Add the broth, wine, Worcestershire, vinegar, rosemary, thyme, oregano, salt, pepper, garlic, onion, carrots and potatoes. Stir, cover, and cook on low 7–8 hours or high 4–5 hours.
3. Ladle ½ cup of the broth into a bowl and whisk in the almond milk and flour until smooth. Stir it back into the slow cooker with the peas and cook uncovered on high 15–20 minutes, until thickened. Season to taste.
