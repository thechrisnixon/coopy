## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **2 tablespoons** olive oil — *A*
- **1 pound** ground chicken breast (or a mix of dark and white) — *A*
- **1 large** carrot, finely chopped — *A*
- **½** onion, finely chopped — *A*
- **2 cloves** garlic, minced — *A*
- **1 tablespoon** chili powder — *A*
- **1 teaspoon** Italian seasoning — *A*
- **1** bay leaf — *A*
- **1 6-ounce can** tomato paste — *A*
- **½ cup** dry white wine — *A*
- **½ cup** evaporated milk — *A*
- **2 8-ounce cans** tomato sauce (or one 16-ounce can) — *A*
- **1½ teaspoons** kosher salt, plus more as needed — *A*
- **½ teaspoon** freshly ground black pepper — *A*
- Calabrian chilis, chopped — *B*
  - *stirred in at the very end, after we've taken Coop's portion out — he doesn't do spicy*
- **1 pound** rigatoni — *A*
- **2 tablespoons** fresh parsley, chopped — *A*
- grated Parmesan, for serving — *A*

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-high and brown the chicken, breaking it up, 5–7 minutes. Add the carrot and onion and cook until tender, 7–8 minutes.
2. Add the garlic, chili powder, Italian seasoning and bay leaf and toast 1–2 minutes, then stir in the tomato paste and cook until it darkens, 1–2 minutes.
3. Pour in the wine and simmer until it's absorbed. Add the evaporated milk and stir constantly until it cooks down.
4. Add the tomato sauce, salt, pepper and ½ cup water. Cover and simmer over medium, stirring now and then, until thick, 45 minutes to 1 hour. Fish out the bay leaf.
5. Take out Coop's portion now, then stir the Calabrian chilis into the rest of the sauce.
6. Meanwhile cook the rigatoni in salted water until al dente, saving some pasta water. Toss the pasta with the sauce, loosening with pasta water as needed. Top with parsley and serve with Parmesan.

## Notes

Pretty good! We triple the sauce and freeze it — it's on our postpartum freezer list.

When we triple, we only use about 2/3 of a bag of pasta for two meals.

Coop's portion comes out before the Calabrian chilis go in; the chili powder in the base isn't hot.
