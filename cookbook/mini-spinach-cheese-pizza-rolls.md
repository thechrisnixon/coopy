# Mini Spinach & Cheese Pizza Rolls

Prep 10m · Cook 18m · Total 28m

Tags: coop, snack, contains-dairy, freezes-well

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **13-16 ounces** pizza dough, at room temperature
- **¾ cup** pizza sauce
- **2 cups** shredded mozzarella cheese
- **1 cup** baby spinach, roughly chopped (or minced broccoli)
- **¼ cup** grated Parmesan cheese

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Grease a muffin tin very well, including the rim around each cup — the cheese bubbles over.
2. On a sheet of parchment, stretch the dough by hand into an 11×16-inch rectangle. If it springs back, let it rest 5 minutes and try again.
3. Spread on the sauce, then scatter the spinach, mozzarella and Parmesan. Roll up fairly tightly from a long side, using the parchment to help.
4. Cut into 12 even slices with a serrated knife — halve it, then halve each piece, and so on, for the most even slices.
5. Set one slice in each muffin cup and bake 22–26 minutes, until the dough is cooked through and the tops are golden.
6. Cool 3–5 minutes in the pan so the cheese firms up, then loosen with a knife if needed and lift out.

## Notes

Makes 12 rolls.

Keeps 3 days in the fridge, or up to 3 months in a freezer bag with the air pressed out. Microwave from frozen 30–60 seconds.

The source lists 18 minutes of cook time but the method says 22–26 minutes in the oven — go by the method.
