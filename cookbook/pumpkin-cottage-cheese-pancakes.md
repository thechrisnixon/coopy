# Pumpkin Cottage Cheese Pancakes

Serves 2 at 1× · Prep 5m · Cook 10m · Total 15m

Tags: coop, breakfast, pancakes, contains-dairy

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **½ cup** cottage cheese
- **½ cup** pumpkin puree
- **2 large** eggs
- **1 teaspoon** vanilla (optional)
- **2 teaspoons** baking powder (aluminum free)
- **1 teaspoon** pumpkin spice
- **1 cup** old-fashioned oats
- butter or oil, for the pan

## Method

1. Add the cottage cheese, pumpkin, eggs, vanilla, baking powder, pumpkin spice and oats to a blender in that order. Blend on high a full minute, until smooth. The batter is very thick — don't thin it.
2. Heat a nonstick griddle or large pan over medium-high and grease it. Scoop ¼ cup of batter per pancake and spread it out with the back of the scoop.
3. Cook 2 minutes, until the edges look set and a few bubbles appear, then flip once and cook 2 minutes more. Don't press them down or they go dense.
4. Serve with your favorite toppings.
