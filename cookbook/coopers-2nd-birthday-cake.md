# Cooper's 2nd Birthday Cake (Strawberry Chantilly)

Serves 12 at 1× · Prep 45m · Cook 30m · Total 2h 15m

Tags: dessert, baking, special, birthday, contains-dairy

## Sources

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

### Vanilla cake

- **13 ounces** bleached cake flour
- **13 ounces** granulated sugar
- **3 teaspoon** baking powder
- **¼ teaspoon** baking soda
- **½ teaspoon** salt
- **8 ounces** unsalted butter, softened
- **2 teaspoons** vanilla extract
- **1 teaspoon** almond extract
- **10 ounces** whole milk, room temperature
- **3 ounces** vegetable oil
- **3 large** eggs, room temperature

### Chantilly cream

- **16 ounces** cream cheese, softened
- **8 ounces** unsalted butter, softened
- **8 ounces** mascarpone, softened
- **22 ounces** powdered sugar, sifted
- **1 teaspoon** almond extract
- **1 Tablespoon** vanilla extract
- **16 ounces** heavy whipping cream

### Berry filling

- **2 cups** fresh or frozen berries
  - *we use strawberries*
- **2½ ounces** sugar
- **1 ounces** cold water
- **1 ounces** cornstarch
- **2 teaspoons** lemon juice

### To decorate

- **2 cups** fresh berries

## Method

1. Cake: bring every cold ingredient to room temperature first — it matters. Heat the oven to 350°F and prepare three 8-inch pans.
2. Combine 4 oz of the milk with the oil. Separately, whisk the other 6 oz milk with the vanilla, almond extract and eggs.
3. In a stand mixer with the paddle, mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt. On the lowest speed, add the butter in small pieces until it looks like coarse sand.
4. Add the milk-and-oil all at once and beat on medium for a full 2 minutes. Then, on low, add the egg mixture in three additions, scraping between, just until combined.
5. Divide among the pans and bake 25–35 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean. Tap each pan once on the counter straight out of the oven. Cool 20 minutes in the pans, turn out, wrap, and freeze at least 1 hour.
6. Filling: simmer the berries with the sugar. Whisk the cornstarch into the cold water, stir it into the bubbling berries with the lemon juice, and cook 1–2 minutes until thick and glossy. Cool completely (spread on a sheet pan to speed it up).
7. Chantilly cream: beat the cream cheese, butter and mascarpone until smooth, then beat in the powdered sugar. In a clean bowl, whip the cream to soft peaks with the vanilla and almond extract — watch it, it goes fast. Fold the cream into the cheese mixture just until combined.
8. Assemble: level the chilled layers. Layer cake, berry filling, cream and fresh berries; repeat; top with the third layer. Crumb-coat and freeze 15 minutes, then frost the outside and smooth with a bench scraper.
9. Pile fresh berries on top and chill at least 30 minutes. Serve cold, or let it sit 15–20 minutes first for the best flavor.

## Notes

The cake Cooper had for his 2nd birthday, made with strawberries.

Start the day before, or at least the morning of: the layers have to cool and freeze before assembly.

Heavy on dairy: cream cheese, mascarpone, butter and cream.
