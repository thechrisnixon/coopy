# Instant Pot Teriyaki Chicken

Serves 4 at 1× · Prep 10m · Cook 20m · Total 40m

Tags: dinner, instant-pot, chicken, freezes-well

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **1½ pounds** boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 3 medium) — *A*
- **⅔ cup** low-sodium soy sauce (or tamari for gluten free) — *A*
- **⅓ cup** water — *A*
- **3 tablespoons** rice vinegar — *A*
- **3 tablespoons** honey — *A*
- **3 tablespoons** brown sugar — *A*
- **2 cloves** garlic, minced — *A*
- **2 tablespoons** fresh ginger, minced — *B*
  - *the recipe's 2 tablespoons is the minimum — add a lot more! We don't measure, just keep going*
- **3 tablespoons** cornstarch, stirred with ¼ cup water — *A*

### For serving

- green onions, thinly sliced (optional) — *A*
- toasted sesame seeds (optional) — *A*
- cooked brown rice, quinoa, noodles, cauliflower rice, or vegetables — *A*

## Method

1. Coat the bottom of the Instant Pot with nonstick spray and lay the chicken in it.
2. Whisk the soy sauce, water, rice vinegar, honey, brown sugar, garlic and plenty of ginger together and pour over the chicken.
3. Seal and cook on HIGH pressure for 10 minutes. Let the pressure release naturally for 5 minutes, then vent the rest.
4. Lift out the chicken and shred it with two forks.
5. Switch to SAUTÉ, whisk in the cornstarch slurry and cook 1–2 minutes until the sauce thickens.
6. Return the chicken to the pot, toss it in the sauce, and serve over rice or whatever you like.

## Notes

We add a lot of ginger — far more than written.

We make 3x and freeze some.
