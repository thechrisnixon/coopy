# Ginger Chicken Meatballs in Peanut Sauce

Serves 4 at 1× · Prep 30m · Cook 30m · Total 1h

Tags: dinner, chicken, spicy

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

### For the meatballs

- **1 pound** 93% lean ground chicken (or lean ground turkey)
- **1** egg
- **½ cup** panko breadcrumbs (or 2 tablespoons coconut flour for GF)
- **¼ cup** green onion, finely chopped
- **¼ cup** cilantro, finely diced
- **1** jalapeño, seeded and finely diced
- **3 cloves** garlic, minced
- **1 tablespoon** fresh ginger, grated
- **¼ teaspoon** cayenne pepper
- **½ teaspoon** salt
- freshly ground black pepper
- **1 tablespoon** toasted sesame oil (or avocado, coconut or olive oil)

### For the peanut sauce

- **1 (15 ounce) can** light coconut milk
- **½ cup** creamy natural peanut butter
- **2 tablespoons** gluten free soy sauce (or coconut aminos)
- **1 tablespoon** chili paste or sriracha

### Add-ins

- **2 large** carrots, thinly sliced
- **1** red bell pepper, julienned
- **⅔ cup** frozen peas (or edamame)

### To garnish

- chopped honey roasted peanuts
- diced scallions
- fresh cilantro
- hot sauce, if desired

## Method

1. Mix the chicken, egg, panko, green onion, cilantro, jalapeño, garlic, ginger, cayenne, salt and pepper by hand and roll into 16 golf-ball-sized meatballs.
2. Heat the sesame oil in a large deep skillet over medium-high and brown the meatballs on all sides, 5–6 minutes total, in batches if needed. Set aside.
3. Lower the heat to medium-low and whisk the coconut milk, peanut butter, soy sauce and chili paste together in the same pan. Add the carrots and the meatballs and bring to a simmer, then cover, turn to low and cook 15 minutes.
4. Uncover, gently stir in the peas and bell pepper, and simmer 5 minutes more. Serve over brown rice, quinoa or zucchini noodles, topped with peanuts, scallions and cilantro.

## Notes

Rich but good. There's plenty of sauce.

For Coop: the heat is in both the meatballs (jalapeño, cayenne) and the sauce (chili paste). Hold back a little of the meat before the jalapeño and cayenne go in for his meatballs, and set aside some sauce before the chili paste is whisked in to simmer them in.
