# Banana Spinach Pancakes

Prep 2m · Cook 10m · Total 20m

Tags: coop, breakfast, pancakes, freezes-well

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **1 ripe** banana
- **½ cup** oats
- **2** eggs
- **½ cup** raw spinach
- **3 tablespoon** water
- coconut or avocado oil, for cooking

## Method

1. Blend everything except the oil until smooth.
2. Heat a skillet over medium heat with a little oil. Drop the batter in tablespoons, about 4 pancakes at a time, and cook for about 3 minutes, until the edges are golden and set enough to flip easily.
3. Flip and cook for another 2 minutes.
4. Serve plain or with toppings — nut butter and berries both work.

## Notes

Makes 12 mini pancakes. Freezes well.

Keeps in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days, or frozen for up to 3 months with parchment between layers. Reheat from frozen in a 180°C / 350°F oven for 5-10 minutes, in the toaster for 1 minute on the defrost setting, or in the microwave for 15 seconds on defrost.
