## Method

1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Whisk the eggs with the milk, scallions, ½ tsp salt and some black pepper.
2. Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment and grease it. Pour in the eggs and bake about 15 minutes, until fully set. Cut into circles with the rim of a glass, or into rectangles for less waste — leftover egg goes into burritos or to the baby.
3. Halve the English muffins, butter the cut sides, and bake 6–8 minutes until just lightly toasted.
4. In a large bowl, mix the ground chicken with the hot honey, garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, 1 tsp salt and a pinch or two of pepper. Stop as soon as it's combined — don't overmix.
5. Coat a large skillet with olive oil over medium heat. Form ½-inch-thick patties the width of an English muffin (they shrink) and cook 5 minutes per side.
6. Assemble: cheese on one muffin half, then the sausage, the egg, and the other half.
7. To freeze, wrap each sandwich in plastic wrap and then foil, and bag them. Reheat in the toaster oven, about 8 minutes.
