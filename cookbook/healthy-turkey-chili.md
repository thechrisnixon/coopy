# Healthy Turkey Chili

Serves 6 at 1× · Prep 10m · Cook 45m · Total 55m

Tags: dinner, turkey

## Sources

From a single source.

**A — Ambitious Kitchen — Seriously, The Best Healthy Turkey Chili**
<https://www.ambitiouskitchen.com/seriously-the-best-healthy-turkey-chili/>
*the whole recipe — lean turkey, kidney beans and corn in a tomato chili*

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **2 teaspoons** olive oil
- **1** yellow onion, chopped
- **3** garlic cloves, minced
- **1 medium** red bell pepper, chopped
- **1 pound** extra lean ground turkey or chicken (99%)
- **4 tablespoons** chili powder
- **2 teaspoons** ground cumin
- **1 teaspoon** dried oregano
- **¼ teaspoon** cayenne pepper
- **½ teaspoon** salt, plus more to taste
- **1 28-ounce can** diced or crushed tomatoes
- **1¼ cups** chicken broth
- **2 15 oz cans** dark red kidney beans, rinsed and drained
- **1 15 oz can** sweet corn, rinsed and drained

### Toppings

- cheese, avocado, tortilla chips, cilantro, sour cream

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-high. Cook the onion, garlic and bell pepper 5–7 minutes, until softened.
2. Add the turkey and cook, breaking it up, until cooked through. Stir in the chili powder, cumin, oregano, cayenne and salt for about 20 seconds.
3. Add the tomatoes, broth, beans and corn. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer 30–45 minutes, until thickened. Adjust the seasoning.
4. Serve with whatever toppings you like.
