# Cauliflower Mac n Cheese

Serves 4 at 1× · Prep 10m · Cook 15m

Tags: coop, dinner, contains-dairy

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **8 oz** elbow pasta
- **2 cups** cauliflower
- **1¼ cups** milk
  - *split — 1/2 cup goes into the cauliflower purée, 3/4 cup into the sauce*
- **1½ cup** cheddar cheese
- **2 tbsp** butter
- **1 tbsp** flour
- **1 tsp** garlic powder
- **1 tsp** dijon mustard
- salt and pepper, to taste

## Method

1. Cook the pasta according to the package directions.
2. Steam the cauliflower.
3. Blend the steamed cauliflower with 1/2 cup of the milk until smooth.
4. Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat, add the flour, and whisk into a roux. Whisk in the remaining 3/4 cup milk and bring to a boil.
5. Turn the heat to low and stir in the cheese, cauliflower purée, garlic powder, dijon, salt and pepper until the cheese has melted.
6. Toss the sauce with the cooked pasta.

## Notes

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for 2-3 days. Reheat on the stovetop or in the microwave.
