# Cooper's Banana Oat Cookies

Cook 10m

Tags: coop, snack, baking

## Sources

From a single source.

**A — @nourishmovelove on Instagram — Easy Healthy Cookie**

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **2 large** ripe bananas, mashed
- **2 cups** rolled oats
- **1 cup** almond butter (the drippy kind), or another nut or seed butter

### Optional

- **1 scoop** whey protein powder
- **1 tbsp – 1/4 cup** ground flaxseed
- **½ cup** chocolate chips
- sea salt, to taste

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
2. Mix everything together until a dough forms.
3. Scoop, flatten slightly, and bake about 10 minutes.

## Notes

A snack Cooper loves. No egg or flour, so he can help and taste the dough.

The bananas need to be properly ripe (spotty). Delivery bananas often arrive green, so buy them a few days before baking.
