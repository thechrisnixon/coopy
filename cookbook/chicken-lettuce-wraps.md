# Chicken Lettuce Wraps

Serves 4 at 1× · Prep 30m · Cook 20m · Total 50m

Tags: dinner, chicken

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

### Sauce

- **3 to 5 tablespoons** hoisin sauce — *A*
  - *start with 3, add more at the end to taste*
- **2 tablespoons** soy sauce — *A*
- **2 tablespoons** rice vinegar — *A*
- **1 teaspoon** toasted sesame oil — *A*
- **1 teaspoon** cornstarch (optional, for a glossier sauce) — *A*
- **1 pound** ground chicken — *A*
- **2 teaspoons** vegetable oil, divided — *A*
- **8 ounces** white button or cremini mushrooms, finely diced — *A*
- **1** bell pepper, thinly sliced — *B*
  - *our addition — goes in with the mushrooms*
- **1** zucchini, diced — *B*
  - *our addition — goes in with the mushrooms*
- **1 (8-ounce) can** water chestnuts, drained and finely chopped — *A*
- **3 cloves** garlic, minced — *A*
- **1 tablespoon** fresh ginger, peeled and minced — *A*
- **½ cup** scallions, thinly sliced (about 6), divided — *A*

### To serve

- **2 small heads** Bibb or butter lettuce — *A*
- sriracha or red pepper flakes, on the side — *A*

## Method

1. Whisk 3 tbsp hoisin with the soy sauce, rice vinegar and sesame oil (and the cornstarch, if using). Keep it by the stove.
2. Heat 1 tsp oil in a large skillet over medium. Cook the chicken, breaking it into small pieces, until no longer pink, 7–8 minutes. Move it to a bowl.
3. Add the remaining 1 tsp oil to the pan. Cook the mushrooms, bell pepper and zucchini, stirring now and then, until tender, 4–5 minutes.
4. Stir in the water chestnuts, garlic and ginger and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
5. Return the chicken with half the scallions, add the sauce, and cook until bubbling and warmed through, 30–60 seconds. Taste and add more hoisin if it needs it.
6. Serve with the lettuce leaves, the rest of the scallions, and sriracha or pepper flakes on the side, for spooning into leaves at the table.

## Notes

We add a thinly sliced bell pepper and a zucchini along with the mushrooms.

The heat is all at the table, so the filling is Coop-friendly as is.

The filling keeps 4 days in the fridge. Reheat just what you need, with a splash of broth if it looks dry.
