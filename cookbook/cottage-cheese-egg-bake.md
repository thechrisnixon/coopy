# Cottage Cheese Egg Bake

Serves 9 at 1× · Prep 5m · Cook 55m · Total 1h

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **12** eggs
- **12-16 oz** turkey breakfast sausage (ground, removed from casing, or chopped pre-cooked)
- **1 tsp** olive oil
- **16 oz** cottage cheese
- **1½ cups** sharp cheddar cheese, shredded
- **½** sweet onion, diced small
- **2 cloves** garlic, minced
- **1** green bell pepper, diced small
- **1** red bell pepper, diced small
- **¼ tsp** salt
- **¼ tsp** black pepper

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 350°.
2. Heat the oil in a medium skillet over medium and cook the sausage through, about 10 minutes. Move it to a plate.
3. In the same skillet, sauté the onion, garlic and peppers 4–5 minutes until soft. Take off the heat.
4. Blend the eggs, cottage cheese, cheddar, salt and pepper until smooth — an immersion blender or a regular one both work. Blending is what keeps it fluffy.
5. Stir in the sausage and vegetables.
6. Pour into a greased 9×13" dish and bake about 40–50 minutes, until firm in the middle. Cover with foil for the last 10 minutes if the top browns too much.
7. Cool at least 10 minutes, then cut into 9–12 pieces.

## Notes

The recipe makes 9–12 pieces. A good make-ahead breakfast.
