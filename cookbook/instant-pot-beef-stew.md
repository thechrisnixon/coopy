## Method

1. Cut the chuck into 1½-inch cubes, trimming large pieces of fat, and season with some of the salt and pepper.
2. On Sauté (high), heat some of the oil and brown the beef in batches, 4–5 minutes per side. Set aside. Prep the vegetables while it browns.
3. Add the remaining oil and the onion and cook until soft, about 6 minutes. Add the garlic for 30 seconds.
4. Pour in the broth and scrape the bottom thoroughly to lift every browned bit.
5. Add the carrots, parsnips, potato, Worcestershire, bay leaf, thyme and the beef. Pour the tomato sauce and diced tomatoes over the top and don't stir.
6. Seal and cook on high pressure for 35 minutes, then let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes before venting.
7. Whisk the cornstarch with the water and stir it in, then add the peas. Serve hot with parsley.
