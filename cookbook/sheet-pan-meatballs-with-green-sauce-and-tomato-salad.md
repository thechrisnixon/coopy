# Sheet Pan Meatballs with Green Sauce and Tomato Salad

Serves 4 at 1× · Prep 15m · Cook 30m · Total 45m

Tags: dinner, sheet-pan, beef, contains-dairy

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

### Green sauce

- **2** garlic cloves
- **1 cup** parsley
- **1 cup** cilantro
- **¼ cup** capers or pitted castelvetrano olives
- **½ cup** walnuts
- juice and zest of a lemon
- **½ cup** olive oil
- salt to taste

### Sheet pan meatballs

- **1** egg
- **1 tablespoon** paprika
- **1 teaspoon** salt
- **1 cup** panko
- **1 lb.** ground beef

### Extras

- **1 small** baguette, for serving
- **1 cup** tzatziki (store-bought)

### Marinated tomato salad

- **1 pound** cherry or grape tomatoes, halved or quartered
- **1 clove** garlic, finely grated
- **2 tablespoons** olive oil
- **1 tablespoon** vinegar or lemon juice
- **1 tablespoon** fresh herbs, minced (parsley)
- salt to taste (about 1/2 – 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt)
- **1** cucumber, sliced

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
2. Toss the tomato salad together and add the sliced cucumber so it marinates while you cook.
3. Pulse the garlic, parsley, cilantro, capers and walnuts in a food processor until finely chopped but not a paste.
4. Put half of that herb base in a large bowl with the egg, paprika, salt, panko and beef and mix well. Roll into balls, set on a sheet pan, brush with olive oil and bake 10–12 minutes.
5. Stir the lemon zest and juice, olive oil, salt and pepper into the other half of the herb base (red pepper flakes optional) to make the green sauce.
6. Serve the meatballs over tzatziki with the bread and tomato salad, drizzled with green sauce.
