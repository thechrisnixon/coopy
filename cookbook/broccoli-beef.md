# Broccoli Beef

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

### Beef and broccoli

- **1 lb** flank steak, very thinly sliced into bite-sized strips
- **2 Tbsp** olive oil (or vegetable oil), divided
- **1 lb** fresh broccoli (about 6 cups of florets)
- **2 tsp** sesame seeds, optional garnish

### Stir fry sauce

- **1 tsp** fresh ginger, grated (loosely packed)
- **2 tsp** garlic, grated (from 3 cloves)
- **½ cup** hot water
- **6 Tbsp** low sodium soy sauce (or GF tamari)
- **3 Tbsp** packed light brown sugar
- **1½ Tbsp** corn starch
- **¼ tsp** black pepper
- **2 Tbsp** sesame oil

## Method

1. Get the rice going first. Freeze the steak for 30 minutes so it slices easily, then cut it thinly against the grain.
2. Stir all the sauce ingredients together until the sugar dissolves.
3. Heat 1 Tbsp oil over medium and cook the broccoli, partly covered and stirring now and then, 4–5 minutes until bright green and crisp-tender. Remove.
4. Turn the heat to high, add the other 1 Tbsp oil, and sear the beef in a single layer, 2 minutes per side, until cooked through.
5. Pour in the sauce, drop to medium-low and simmer 3–4 minutes until thick. Return the broccoli and toss, adding 1–2 Tbsp water if it's too thick. Serve over rice with sesame seeds.
