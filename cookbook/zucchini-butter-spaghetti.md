# Zucchini Butter Spaghetti

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **1½ teaspoons** kosher salt, plus more for the pasta water and to taste
- **8 ounces** spaghetti (225 grams)
- **1 cup** pasta water, reserved (235 ml)
- **1¼ pounds** zucchini, trimmed and coarsely grated (570 grams)
- **6 tablespoons** unsalted butter (85 grams)
- **4** garlic cloves, minced
- **¼ teaspoon** red pepper flakes, or more to taste
- **½ cup** grated parmesan (45 grams)
- handful of fresh basil leaves, cut into thin ribbons

## Method

1. Cook the spaghetti in well-salted boiling water until 1 minute short of done. Save 1 cup of the pasta water, then drain.
2. Meanwhile, melt the butter in a large deep skillet over medium-high. Add the garlic and stir for 1 minute.
3. Add the zucchini, salt and red pepper flakes. Cook 13–15 minutes, stirring now and then, until the liquid has cooked off and the zucchini is soft.
4. Keep cooking 2 more minutes, stirring often and breaking the zucchini up with the spoon until it's almost a spread.
5. Add 1/2 cup of the pasta water to loosen the pan, then add the drained spaghetti and cook together for 2 minutes, tossing with tongs so the zucchini clings to the strands. Add more pasta water if it's tight.
6. Toss in half the parmesan and basil, then top with the rest to serve.

## Notes

We serve it with simple chicken on the side.

Contains dairy (butter and parmesan).

The recipe says it serves 2 generously or 4 lightly.
