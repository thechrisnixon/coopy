# Blueberry Carrot Oatmeal Bars

Prep 10m · Cook 24m · Total 34m

Tags: coop, snack

## Sources

From a single source.

**A — Yummy Toddler Food — Easy Oatmeal Bars (with Blueberry and Carrot)**
<https://www.yummytoddlerfood.com/easy-oatmeal-bars/>
*the whole recipe — one-bowl oat bars with almond butter, maple and grated carrot*

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **¾ cup** quick or instant oats (or rolled oats ground in a blender)
- **½ cup** whole wheat flour
- **½ teaspoon** baking powder
- **½ teaspoon** cinnamon
- **¼ teaspoon** salt
- **¼ cup** melted coconut oil (or butter)
- **¼ cup** creamy unsweetened almond butter
- **¼ cup** maple syrup
- **1** egg (optional)
  - *without it the bars are crisp like a cookie; with it they're cakier*
- **½ cup** grated carrot
- **½ cup** blueberries, halved if very large

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Line an 8×8-inch pan with parchment, leaving an overhang on two sides to lift the bars out later.
2. Mix everything except the blueberries in a medium bowl (egg too, if using), then gently fold in the berries.
3. Press evenly into the pan, right to the edges.
4. Bake 24–26 minutes, until the edges are lightly golden and set.
5. Let it cool completely — the fridge speeds this up. Cut warm and it crumbles.
6. Lift out and cut into 16 small squares, or larger if you like.

## Notes

Makes 16 small bars. Good made ahead.
