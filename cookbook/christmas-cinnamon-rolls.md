# Christmas Cinnamon Rolls

Serves 9 at 1× · Prep 2h · Cook 20m · Total 2h 20m

Tags: breakfast, baking, contains-dairy, special, holiday

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

### Dough

- **¾ cup** milk (whole or 2%), 180 g
- **2¼ teaspoons** quick-rise (instant) or active dry yeast (one ¼-ounce package)
- **¼ cup** granulated sugar (50 g)
- **4 tablespoons** salted butter, melted and cooled (57 g)
- **1 large** egg, room temperature
- **1 large** egg yolk, room temperature
- **3 cups** bread flour (360 g), plus more as needed
- **¾ teaspoon** kosher salt
- extra-virgin olive oil, for greasing the bowl

### Filling

- **4 tablespoons** salted butter, softened (57 g)
- **⅔ cup** packed dark brown sugar (142 g)
- **1½ tablespoons** ground cinnamon

### Cream cheese frosting

- **4 oz** cream cheese, softened (112 g)
- **¾ cup** powdered sugar (85 g)
- **3 tablespoons** salted butter, softened
- **½ teaspoon** vanilla extract

## Method

1. Warm the milk to 115°F (45 seconds to 1 minute in the microwave) and pour it into a stand mixer bowl fitted with the paddle. Sprinkle the yeast over, add the sugar, melted butter, egg and yolk, and mix on medium for 30 seconds.
2. Stir in the flour and salt with a wooden spoon until a dough forms. Switch to the dough hook and knead on medium 8–10 minutes until soft and slightly sticky, adding flour a tablespoon at a time only if needed.
3. Move the dough to an oiled bowl, cover with plastic wrap and a warm towel, and let it double, 1–1½ hours.
4. On a well-floured surface, roll it to a 10 × 14-inch rectangle. Spread with the softened butter, leaving a ¼-inch strip bare along one short side. Mix the brown sugar and cinnamon, scatter it over, and rub it gently into the butter.
5. Roll up tightly from the other short side and pinch the seam closed. Seam side down, trim ½ inch off each end and nudge the ends in to compact the log. Cut into nine 1-inch rolls with floss or a serrated knife.
6. Set the rolls in a parchment-lined 9 × 9 or 11 × 7-inch pan, cover, and let rise until doubled and touching, 45 minutes to 1 hour. Heat the oven to 350°F.
7. Bake uncovered until golden at the edges, 20–25 minutes. Cool 10 minutes.
8. Beat the cream cheese, powdered sugar, butter and vanilla on low for 30 seconds, then medium-high 1–2 minutes until fluffy. Spread over the warm rolls.

## Notes

Our Christmas morning cinnamon rolls. Makes 9 large rolls.

Start early — there's about 2 hours of rising before they bake.
