# Chicken Strips

Prep 30m · Cook 8m · Total 38m

Tags: dinner, chicken, contains-dairy

*the recipe — ground chicken bound with milk-soaked bread, double breaded*

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

### Main

- **1 slice** bread
- **¼ cup** milk
- **500 grams** ground chicken

### Seasonings

- **1 teaspoon** onion powder (or 2 tablespoons finely chopped onion, excess water squeezed out)
- **½ teaspoon** garlic powder (or 1 teaspoon garlic paste)
- **1½ teaspoon** chicken powder (or ½ bouillon cube mixed with a little cornflour)
- **2 tablespoon** lemon juice
- **½ teaspoon** salt, more to taste
- **½ teaspoon** pepper

### To coat

- **2 large** eggs, for egg wash
- **3 cup** bread crumb
- oil, to fry

## Method

1. Soak the bread in the milk, then mash it.
2. Mix the mashed bread, seasonings and lemon juice into the ground chicken and chill for 15 minutes.
3. Whisk the eggs with a little salt and pepper; spread the breadcrumbs on a plate.
4. With oiled hands, portion the mixture, roll it in breadcrumbs and shape into strips. Dip in egg wash, then coat in breadcrumbs again.
5. Pan fry in a cast iron skillet in hot oil over medium heat, 2–3 minutes, then turn the heat up for about 2 minutes more until golden.
6. Move them to the air fryer for about 5 minutes at 400°F to finish cooking through and crisp up. Serve with a dip or ketchup.

## Notes

The source makes these as nuggets (about 20 pieces per batch); we shape them into strips.

Our method: pan fry in cast iron, then finish in the air fryer for about 5 minutes at 400°F.
