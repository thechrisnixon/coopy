# Spicy Turkey and Green Bean Stir-fry

Prep 5m · Cook 15m · Total 20m

Tags: dinner, turkey, spicy

## Ingredients

*Quantities are 1× — one batch, exactly as the source wrote them.*

- **1 lb** green beans — *A*
- **4 tsp** coconut oil (or vegetable oil) — *A*
- **1 tbsp** sesame oil — *A*
- **2** garlic cloves, minced — *A*
- **2 tbsp** ginger, minced — *A*
- **1½ lb** 99% lean ground turkey — *B*
  - *the recipe says 1.33 lb — we make a full 1 1/2 lb so there's more to go around*
- **8 tbsp** low sodium soy sauce (GF if needed, or coconut aminos) — *B*
  - *double the recipe's 4 tbsp — we always double the sauce*
- **4 tbsp** rice vinegar — *B*
  - *doubled from 2 tbsp, along with the rest of the sauce*
- **4 tsp** Asian chili garlic paste (like sambal oelek) — *B*
  - *doubled from 2 tsp with the rest of the sauce — this is all the heat, so it goes in after Coop's portion comes out*

## Method

1. Heat the broiler. Toss the green beans with half the coconut oil, spread them on a foil-lined sheet pan and broil 6–8 minutes, shaking the pan once, until tender and charred in spots.
2. Meanwhile, heat the rest of the coconut oil and the sesame oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add the turkey, garlic and ginger and cook, breaking it up, until browned and cooked through.
3. Add the broiled green beans, the soy sauce and the rice vinegar and toss to coat. Scoop out Coop's portion now.
4. Stir in the chili garlic paste and cook 1 minute. Taste and add more soy sauce if it needs it.

## Notes

Ease: 1/6 (our rating, 1 = easiest).

We double the sauce and use a full 1 1/2 lb of turkey instead of the recipe's 1.33 lb — the quantities above already include both changes.

The chili garlic paste is the only heat. Pull Coop's portion after the soy sauce and vinegar go in, before the chili paste.

The recipe says it serves 2–3 (1 1/2–2 cups each), before our changes.
